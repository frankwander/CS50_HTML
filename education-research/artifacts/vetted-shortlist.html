01 — The spine

Assessment & long-term academic oversight

The purchase that actually answers "is he being stretched?" — an objective cognitive baseline now, then periodic academic benchmarking through primary, held together by one standing advisor. This is the part a state school won't do.

⚠︎ Get the test right: at four it's the WPPSI-IV, not the WISC-V

The widely-quoted WISC-V only starts at age 6. A four-year-old is assessed on the WPPSI-IV (ages 2y6m–7y7m). Any assessor offering to "WISC" him now is being loose with terminology — confirm they use the age-appropriate instrument before booking.

Now · age 4–5

Cognitive baseline

One objective read from an educational psychologist (WPPSI-IV) or Potential Plus Early Years.

Annual / biennial

Academic benchmark

Consultancy assessment placing him vs peers & target schools; repeat as a check.

Ongoing

Standing advisor

One retained consultant/mentor steering enrichment & the 7+/11+ decision.

If picture shifts

EP re-test

Only re-assess with a psychologist if something materially changes.
