## The structural problem

The Belham (Charter Schools Educational Trust) has a **two-week** autumn break:
**Mon 19 Oct – Fri 30 Oct 2026**.

But the **standard Southwark state-school half term is 26–30 Oct** — week two. So in
**week one (19–23 Oct)** most local state schools are still in session, and most camps aimed at
them don't run. Week one is the scarce week.

**The workaround:** the Dulwich independent schools also break for a fortnight, and their
calendars cover week one.
