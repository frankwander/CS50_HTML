## Sailing — not possible at 5

Every London centre has a minimum age of **8** (some 9–10). This is the RYA junior pathway's age
floor, not a booking problem. Surrey Docks 8 · AHOY Deptford 8 · Docklands instructed 8 ·
Shadwell Basin 9 · Westminster Boating Base 10 · Queen Mary / Welsh Harp / King George / Tamesis 8.

**Age-5 water alternatives:**
- **GoBoat** — self-drive electric picnic boats, **all ages** (driver 18+), buoyancy aids provided,
  **runs weekdays**, Canary Wharf nearest. The only option where he stays **dry and warm** — which
  in late October matters.
- **Royal Parks boating** — Regent's Park child-sized pedalos, daily through October, closes at dusk
- **Docklands equipment hire** — genuine **age-5 minimum** (5–7s share with someone 8+), but October
  non-member access is **weekends only**: Fri 23 ❌, **Sat 24 ✅**
- **Moo Canoes** (Limehouse) — no real minimum with an adult aboard; **24 Oct is the last day of
  their season**
- **Shadwell Basin junior programme** (5–8 with parents, ~£4/session) — but the child **must swim
  confidently in deep water**, a real bar at 5. ☎️ 020 7481 4210

**Sailing unlocks at age 8** — that opens nearly everything at once, including **AHOY's 8–14
holiday club in Deptford, ~£300/week, 20 min from Peckham**. Age 7 gets Datchet Water or Thames
YC, both outside London.
