## 1. Assessment & long-term oversight (the spine)

### ⚠️ Get the test right
The widely-quoted **WISC-V starts at age 6**. A four-year-old is assessed on the **WPPSI-IV**
(ages 2y6m–7y7m). Any assessor offering to "WISC" him now is being loose with terminology.
Several clinics quoting ~£900–£950 default to the WISC-V battery — so their standard product
is not for him yet.

### Suggested spine
WPPSI-IV baseline (EP or Potential Plus Early Years) at 4–5 → annual/biennial academic
benchmark via one consultancy → EP re-test only if the picture changes. Keep one consultancy as
the standing "what next" advisor into the 7+/11+ decision.

### Gifted / high learning potential specialists

- **Potential Plus UK** ★★ — national charity since 1967, **impartial** (not selling tuition).
  Its **Early Years Assessment of Potential (ages 3–4)** is purpose-built for exactly this
  question. Family membership gives ongoing guidance on stretching an able child and pushing
  school for challenge. **In-person at Milton Keynes** (~1hr by train) — the one geographic
  caveat. HLP assessment £620 members / £750 non-members; Early Years priced separately.
- **Mensa Gifted Child** / **Advanced Assessments** — parent consultations, workshops,
  G&T programme design. Online/national. Supplementary rather than primary.

## 3. All-round (music, drama, sport)

*Note: the dedicated agent for this strand hit an API limit mid-run. Compiled from other
research — treat all prices as "verify".*

### Music
- **Centre for Young Musicians (CYM)** ★ — London's flagship Saturday music school, Morley
  College (Waterloo). Ages 5–18, must play an instrument. The credential to aim toward.
- **Junior Guildhall / Junior Trinity / Junior RAM** — audition-standard conservatoire junior
  departments. The top of the youth-music tree.
- **SE22 Piano School** ★ — **Local** (East Dulwich/Peckham Rye), Sat mornings, explicitly
  prepares children for **music scholarships and aptitude tests**.
- **Dulwich Piano Lessons** — local Saturday tuition, ages 5+.

### Drama & confidence
- **Theatre Peckham** ★ — **Local, SE15.** 28+ years, ages 3–18. Acting, singing, musical
  theatre, dance. Confidence and articulacy pay off directly in 11+ interviews.
- **Stagecoach Dulwich** — ages 4–18, weekends.
- **Perform** — ages 4–10, at Dulwich College Sports Ground. LAMDA route later.

### Sport
- **We Make Footballers Peckham** ★ — **Local.** Ages 4–12, weekly + holiday camps.
- **Dulwich College Sports Club** — ages 5–14. 25m pool, astro, tennis, athletics track.
  Junior tennis camps ~£100 per 4-day camp.
- **Swimming — Southwark GLL/Better** — learn-to-swim from ~4, holiday intensives.
